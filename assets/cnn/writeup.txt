================================================================================
  (Re-)Imag(in)ing Price Trends: A China A-Share Replication
  Research Summary
================================================================================


1. PROBLEM SETUP
----------------
This project replicates and extends Jiang, Kelly & Xiu (2023) — hereafter
JKX — to China A-shares. The original paper demonstrates that convolutional
neural networks (CNNs) trained on stock price chart images generate powerful
out-of-sample return predictions for U.S. equities, substantially outperforming
traditional price trend signals such as momentum and short-term reversal.

We ask whether the same image-based predictability holds in China, a market
with distinct microstructure features including daily price limits (10% up/down),
a predominantly retail investor base, and different information environments
relative to the U.S.

The prediction task is binary classification: given a stock's price chart image
over the past I days (I = 5, 20, 60), predict whether the subsequent R-day
return (R = 5, 20, 60) is positive or negative. This yields nine model
specifications denoted Ix/Ry.

The out-of-sample test period spans 2013–2024, with rolling training windows
beginning in 2005. All results are fully out-of-sample.


2. MODEL ARCHITECTURE
---------------------
Three model families are evaluated:

2D CNN (Image CNN)
  Input: black-and-white OHLC chart image with volume bars and moving average.
  Architecture: stacked convolutional blocks (2/3/4 blocks for I5/I20/I60),
  each consisting of 5×3 convolution → leaky ReLU → 2×1 max-pooling.
  Filter counts: 64, 128, 256, 512 (doubling per layer). Fully connected
  output layer with softmax activation producing P(up). 50% dropout on FC
  layer. Early stopping with patience = 3 epochs. Base filters = 64.
  This is the paper's primary model.

1D CNN (Time-Series CNN)
  Uses the same image-scaled numerical features (open, high, low, close,
  moving average, volume — all normalised to [0,1] by the image-scale method)
  but applies 1D convolutional filters that slide only along the time dimension.
  Eliminates the 2D spatial structure while retaining the key normalisation.

Logistic Regression
  Linear classifier applied to the same image-scaled 1D feature vectors.
  Trained with L2 regularisation (C=1.0), saga solver, balanced 50/50 labels.
  Serves as the interpretable baseline approximating the CNN.

All models share identical training protocol: rolling windows
(2005–2012 train → 2013–2015 test; 2005–2015 → 2016–2018; 2005–2018 →
2019–2021; 2005–2021 → 2022–2024), 70/30 train/validation split within each
window, balanced class labels.


3. DATA PIPELINE
----------------
Universe: All China A-share stocks with ≥ 180 days of listing history.

Price data: Adjusted OHLC prices and volume from CSMAR (clean_data.parquet).
  Forward returns: adj_close[T+h] / adj_close[T] − 1, computed at the
  prediction date T, consistent with the binary label used in training.

Image construction (replicating JKX Section I):
  Each day occupies 3 pixel columns (open mark, high-low bar, close mark).
  Moving average length = image length (e.g., 20-day MA for I20 images).
  Volume occupies bottom 20% of image height. All prices normalised so that
  max(high) = 1 and min(low) = 0 within each window (image-scale normalisation).
  Pixel dimensions: I5 → 32×15, I20 → 64×60, I60 → 96×180.
  1D features: same six normalised series stored as float32 arrays.

Exclusions on prediction date:
  Stocks hitting the upper price limit (涨停, LimitStatus = 1) are excluded
  from portfolio entry, consistent with China market microstructure.


4. PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION AND EVALUATION
-----------------------------------------
Methodology: Non-overlapping rebalancing every h trading days (matching JKX).
  R5 → ~weekly (~50 periods/year, N=583 over test sample)
  R20 → ~monthly (~12 periods/year, N=146)
  R60 → ~quarterly (~4 periods/year, N=49)

Decile portfolios: Stocks ranked by P(up) each rebalancing date, assigned to
  deciles 1 (lowest signal) through 10 (highest signal). Equal-weighted.

Long-short (H-L): Long decile 10, short decile 1.

Annualisation: mean(period_ret) × (252/h); std × sqrt(252/h).

Statistical inference: Newey-West t-statistics with 4 lags, correcting for
  potential serial correlation in period returns.

Transaction costs (Frazzini, Israel & Moskowitz 2018, simplified):
  10 bps one-way, flat across all stocks.
  Decile portfolios: 20 bps round-trip per period (buy + sell).
  Long-short: 40 bps round-trip per period (both legs).
  Cost drag annualised: ~10.1% for R5, ~2.5% for R20, ~0.8% for R60.


5. KEY RESULTS
--------------
Table 1 (R5, Weekly) — headline results:

  Strategy        L/S Sharpe (Gross)   L/S Sharpe (Net)   Ann. Ret Gross
  1D CNN  I5           5.41                 3.28              51.1%
  1D CNN I20           5.28                 3.23              52.0%
  1D CNN I60           4.69                 2.69              47.4%
  2D CNN  I5           4.85                 2.98              52.3%
  Logistic I5          2.97                 1.41              38.4%
  Logistic I20         2.76                 1.11              33.8%
  Logistic I60         2.51                 0.78              29.2%

Table 2 (R20, Monthly):

  Strategy        L/S Sharpe (Gross)   L/S Sharpe (Net)   Ann. Ret Gross
  1D CNN  I5           2.57                 2.02              23.7%
  2D CNN  I5           2.38                 1.85              22.4%
  1D CNN I20           2.11                 1.62              21.7%
  1D CNN I60           2.01                 1.45              18.1%
  Logistic I60         1.12                 0.74              14.7%

Table 3 (R60, Quarterly):

  Strategy        L/S Sharpe (Gross)   L/S Sharpe (Net)
  1D CNN  I5           1.42                 1.21
  1D CNN I20           1.30                 1.10
  2D CNN  I5           1.05                 0.85
  Logistic I60         0.91                 0.77

Decile monotonicity: CNN models produce strongly monotonic decile return
  profiles at R5 and R20 — decile 1 is deeply negative (−19.5% annualised at
  R5 for 1D CNN I5) and decile 10 strongly positive (+29–34%). Logistic
  regression profiles are flatter, with compressed D6–D10 spread, particularly
  at longer horizons where D6–D10 returns converge.

Long-only (D10 gross/net):
  Best gross: 1D CNN I20/R5 at 34.4% annualised.
  Best net:   1D CNN I20/R5 at 24.3% (after 10.1% annualised cost drag).
  At R60, net D10 returns remain attractive (21.8% for 1D CNN I5) due to
  negligible quarterly cost drag (~0.8% annualised), making quarterly
  strategies particularly cost-efficient on a long-only basis.

Market benchmark: The equal-weight market index (long-only reference) is
  largely flat in cumulative terms across R5 and R20, confirming the L/S
  and long-only returns are not driven by market beta.


6. CONCLUSIONS
--------------
(i)  Image-based CNN predictions are strongly profitable in China A-shares,
     consistent with JKX's U.S. findings. Gross L/S Sharpe ratios of 5.4
     (weekly) and 2.6 (monthly) substantially exceed any plausible benchmark.
     The decile return profiles are strongly monotonic for CNN models, with
     D1 earning −19.5% and D10 earning +34.4% annualised at R5 (1D CNN I20).

(ii) 1D CNN matches or outperforms 2D CNN across all specifications on a
     Sharpe basis. This replicates JKX's Table IX finding: the key driver of
     performance is image-scale normalisation, not the spatial 2D convolution.
     Once data are image-scaled, 1D convolution is sufficient.

(iii) CNN dominates logistic regression at R5 and R20 by a wide margin
     (Sharpe 5.4 vs 3.0 at R5). The gap narrows at R60, consistent with
     JKX's finding that longer horizons are more accessible to linear models.
     Notably, Logistic I60 achieves a competitive gross Sharpe of 0.91 at R60,
     nearly matching 1D CNN I60 (0.93).

(iv) Transaction costs matter most at R5. The net Sharpe drops from 5.4 to
     3.3 for weekly CNN strategies (~10.1% annualised cost drag). At R20 the
     drag is only ~2.5%, and at R60 ~0.8%, making lower-frequency strategies
     more practically viable. The best net L/S Sharpe overall is 1D CNN I5/R5
     at 3.28, while the best net long-only D10 return is 1D CNN I20/R5 at 24.3%.

(v)  Shorter image windows (I5) generally outperform longer ones (I20, I60)
     at R5, but the advantage diminishes at longer rebalancing horizons.
     This is consistent with JKX's finding that image-based predictions are
     most potent in the short horizon following image observance.

(vi) China A-share results broadly confirm JKX but with some differences.
     The gross Sharpe ratios (5.4 weekly, 2.6 monthly) are somewhat lower than
     JKX's U.S. results (7.2, 2.4) but remain economically large. The stronger
     performance at short horizons relative to long horizons is consistent with
     the microstructure of China A-shares, where retail-driven momentum and
     reversal effects are concentrated at weekly frequencies.
