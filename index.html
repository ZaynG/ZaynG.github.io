Inspired by Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2020)  ·  Chinese A-shares  ·  2002–2025
Can Machines Learn to Trade Momentum?
The classical 12-1 momentum rule fails in Chinese A-shares. This project tests whether machine learning models can recover cross-sectional return predictability from price and volume data across 5,432 stocks. XGBoost achieves a long-short Sharpe ratio of 2.04 — where naive momentum produces −0.10.

Inspired by Jiang, Kelly & Xiu (2023)  ·  Chinese A-shares  ·  2005–2024
Can CNNs Read Price Charts in the Chinese Equity Market?
Extends the image-based CNN return prediction framework to Chinese equities, a market with daily price limits, a retail-dominated investor base, and weaker information efficiency. A 1D CNN achieves a gross long-short Sharpe ratio of 5.41 at weekly frequency across 1.2 million stock-weeks.

Inspired by López de Prado (2016)  ·  S&P 500 & CSI 300  ·  1996–2025
Hierarchical Risk Parity Across U.S. and Chinese Equities
Evaluates whether hierarchical clustering-based portfolio construction outperforms other risk parity strategies — inverse volatility weighting and equal risk contribution — in both U.S. and Chinese equity markets. HRP achieves the highest Sharpe ratio (0.87) and shallowest maximum drawdown (−43.7%) among five strategies on the S&P 500 backtest spanning 28 years.
